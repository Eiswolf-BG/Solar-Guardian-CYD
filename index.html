SOLAR GUARDIAN CYD

Firmware Flash Utility
Version: v1.8

Install the Solar Guardian firmware directly from your browser to your Cheap Yellow Display.

1. Connect your CYD via USB cable
2. Click the "Connect" button below
3. Select the correct COM port
4. Sit back and wait for the process to finish
5. An SD card (FAT32) is required for first-time setup
Requires Chrome, Edge, or Opera (Web Serial API)
